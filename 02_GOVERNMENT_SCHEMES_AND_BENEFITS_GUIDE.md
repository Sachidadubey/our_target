## 1. National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS 2.0) — Direct DBT Stipend

* **Administering Body:** Ministry of Education (Department of Higher Education) & Ministry of Skill Development, Govt. of India.
* **Direct Official Portal:** [https://nats.education.gov.in](https://nats.education.gov.in)
* **What Financial Benefit & Leverage It Gives You:**
  * **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT):** The Government of India pays **₹4,500/month directly into your Aadhaar-linked bank account**. The recruiting Bank / PSU pays the remaining portion.
  * *Examples:*
    * **Canara Bank:** Pays ₹10,500 + Govt DBT ₹4,500 = **₹15,000/month** total stipend.
    * **DRDO / SAIL / BEL:** Pays total stipend of **₹9,000 – ₹12,000/month**.
  * **Certificate of Proficiency:** Issued upon 1-year completion by the Ministry of Education. Under Government rules, this certificate is legally treated as **1 year of formal technical work experience** when applying for lateral/technical posts in PSUs (like BEL, HAL, ECIL) that ask for "1 year experience".
* **Exact Eligibility:**
  * Must hold a 4-year B.E. / B.Tech degree in Engineering (Computer Science & IT).
  * Must have passed out within the last 3 years (2024, 2025, or 2026).
  * Must NOT have prior formal job training or prior apprenticeship contract.
* **Documents Required for Registration:**
  1. Aadhaar Card (Must be linked to your active bank account for DBT).
  2. B.Tech Degree Certificate or Provisional Certificate or Final Year Consolidated Marksheet.
  3. Active Bank Account details (Account Number & IFSC Code).
  4. Scanned Passport Photo (JPEG < 200 KB) and Signature (< 100 KB).
* **Step-by-Step Application Process:**
  1. Go to [https://nats.education.gov.in](https://nats.education.gov.in).
  2. Click **"Student"** $\rightarrow$ **"Register"**.
  3. Enter your Aadhaar number; authenticate via OTP.
  4. Fill in your educational details (Engineering $\rightarrow$ Computer Science/IT $\rightarrow$ College Name $\rightarrow$ Percentage/CGPA).
  5. Upload your marksheet and submit. Your NATS Student Enrollment ID will be generated within 24–48 hours.
  6. Once approved, log in, navigate to **"Establishment Requests"** $\rightarrow$ Search for active establishments (e.g., Canara Bank, SAIL Bhilai, DRDO) $\rightarrow$ Click **"Apply"**.

## 2. Digital India Internship Scheme — Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY)

* **Administering Body:** Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Govt. of India.
* **Direct Official Portal:** [https://intern.meity.gov.in](https://intern.meity.gov.in)
* **What Financial Benefit & Leverage It Gives You:**
  * **Stipend:** **₹10,000/month** for a period of 2 months (Total = **₹20,000**).
  * **Government Placement:** Placed directly inside MeitY divisions, National Informatics Centre (NIC), CERT-In (Computer Emergency Response Team), or IndiaAI.
  * **Certificate:** Official Internship Certificate signed by the Secretary, MeitY. Exceptional value on your resume for both public sector technical interviews (SEBI, ISRO) and high-tier private tech firms.
* **Exact Eligibility:**
  * Indian students pursuing B.Tech in CSE / IT in 3rd/4th year or fresh graduates with minimum 60% marks in aggregate.
* **Work Domains Available:**
  * Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning.
  * Cybersecurity & Threat Intelligence.
  * Digital Personal Data Protection & Privacy.
  * Cloud Computing & Infrastructure (MeghRaj cloud).
  * Web & Mobile Application Development for National Portals.
* **Selection Process:** Academic merit (B.Tech percentage) + Project evaluation (your MERN stack, Java, REST APIs, or AI projects). Shortlisted candidates may attend an online virtual interview.
* **Application Cycles:**
  * **Winter Batch:** Opens in **October / November** each year.
  * **Summer Batch:** Opens in **April / May** each year.
* **Documents Required:**
  * College recommendation / Bonafide letter or Degree certificate.
  * Curriculum Vitae (CV) emphasizing software/coding projects.
  * Academic marksheets (10th, 12th, B.Tech).

## 3. IndiaAI Fellowship & GPU Compute Infrastructure Grant

* **Administering Body:** IndiaAI Mission, Digital India Corporation (₹10,372 Crore Cabinet-Approved Initiative).
* **Direct Official Portal:** [https://indiaai.gov.in](https://indiaai.gov.in)
* **What Financial Benefit & Leverage It Gives You:**
  * **Student Fellowships:** Grants of **₹15,000 to ₹25,000/month** for engineering graduates working on AI/ML research or open-source software applications.
  * **GPU Compute Access:** Subsidized or completely free access to India's National AI Computing Grid (including AIRAWAT and high-performance NVIDIA H100 GPU clusters) for training deep learning models.
  * **Career Leverage:** Direct networking with chief technology officers of Indian government bodies, IIT researchers, and national digital mission directors.
* **Exact Eligibility:**
  * B.Tech CSE graduates / students with working proficiency in Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, NLP, or computer vision.
* **How to Apply:** Monitor the "Fellowships & Compute Grants" call on [https://indiaai.gov.in](https://indiaai.gov.in). Submit a technical project proposal highlighting national problem statements (e.g., Indic language translation, agricultural image analysis, health data processing).

## 4. FutureSkills PRIME — Government Course Fee Reimbursement Scheme

* **Administering Body:** MeitY & NASSCOM (National Skilling Ecosystem).
* **Direct Official Portal:** [https://futureskillsprime.in](https://futureskillsprime.in)
* **What Financial Benefit & Leverage It Gives You:**
  * **Cashback / Reimbursement:** The Government of India reimburses **up to 50% of the training course fee (Direct Bank Transfer of ₹8,000 to ₹12,000)** upon passing the National Diagnostic Assessment.
  * **Industry-Standard Certification:** Receive government-backed certifications co-branded by MeitY, NASSCOM, and industry leaders (Microsoft, AWS, Google Cloud, Cisco).
* **Eligible Technology Tracks:**
  * Cloud Computing & DevOps Architecture.
  * Cyber Security & Ethical Hacking.
  * Big Data Engineering & SQL Analytics.
  * Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning.
* **Eligibility & Conditions:**
  * Must be an Indian citizen with a recognized engineering degree (B.Tech CSE).
  * Must register on the FutureSkills PRIME portal using your Aadhaar number.
  * Complete an approved course and pass the government-administered proctored test.
  * Govt incentive is credited directly to your bank account via DBT.

## 5. 10% Economically Weaker Section (EWS) Reservation Scheme

* **Administering Body:** Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions.
* **Legal Basis:** 103rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 2019.
* **What Massive Leverage It Gives You:**
  * **10% Vertical Reservation:** 10% of total vacancies across **every Central Government and State Government exam** are strictly reserved for EWS candidates (SSC CGL, RRB NTPC, IBPS PO/SO, SBI, SEBI, UPSC CSE, DRDO, ISRO).
  * **Dramatic Cutoff Advantage:** EWS cutoffs are substantially lower than General (UR) cutoffs:
    * *SSC CGL Tier-1:* UR = 153.28 vs **EWS = 143.44** (9.84 marks lower!).
    * *RRB NTPC Station Master (CBT-2):* UR = 74.08 vs **EWS = 63.85** (10.23 marks lower!).
    * *UPSC Civil Services Prelims:* UR = 75.41 vs **EWS = 68.02** (7.39 marks lower!).
    * *IBPS SO IT Officer Mains:* UR = 14.50 vs **EWS = 11.25** (3.25 marks lower!).
* **Exact Income & Asset Criteria Checklist (Must Satisfy ALL):**
  1. Family gross annual income from all sources (salary, agriculture, business, profession) is **below ₹8 Lakhs** for the financial year prior to application.
  2. Family does not own or possess **5 acres of agricultural land** and above.
  3. Family does not own a **residential flat of 1000 sq. ft.** and above.
  4. Family does not own a **residential plot of 100 sq. yards** and above in notified municipalities.
  5. Family does not own a **residential plot of 200 sq. yards** and above in non-notified areas.
* **Authorized Issuing Authorities:**
  * District Magistrate (DM) / Additional DM / Collector.
  * Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) / Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM).
  * Tehsildar (Executive Magistrate / Revenue Officer).
* **Validity Period:** An EWS certificate is valid for **one financial year** (e.g., certificate issued in FY 2026–2027 is based on income of FY 2025–2026).
* **How to Apply:** Apply through your state e-District online portal (e.g., e-District Delhi, Jharsewa Jharkhand, RTPS Bihar, MP e-District, Seva Sindhu Karnataka) or physically at your local Tehsil office. Fee is nominal (₹15 to ₹50).

## 6. Central Sector Interest Subsidy Scheme (CSIS) for Education Loans

* **Administering Body:** Ministry of Education, Govt. of India.
* **Portal Link:** [https://canarabank.com/pages/csis](https://canarabank.com/pages/csis) (Administered via Canara Bank Nodal Cell).
* **What Financial Benefit It Gives You:**
  * **100% Full Interest Waiver:** If you availed an education loan for your B.Tech under the Indian Banks' Association (IBA) model from any public or scheduled bank, the Central Government pays **all accrued interest during your entire course period + 1 additional year moratorium**.
  * Saves you **₹1.50 to ₹3.50 Lakhs** in accumulated interest.
* **Eligibility:**
  * Enrolled in an AICTE / UGC approved professional degree (B.Tech).
  * Gross parental / family annual income from all sources is **up to ₹4.50 Lakhs/year**.
* **How to Claim:**
  * Submit your family Income Certificate issued by an authorized state authority (Tehsildar/SDO) to the branch manager of the bank where your education loan was sanctioned. The bank uploads your claim to the government CSIS portal.

## 7. Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS) for Tech Entrepreneurs

* **Administering Body:** Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce & Industry.
* **Direct Official Portal:** [https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in](https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in)
* **What Financial Benefit & Leverage It Gives You:**
  * **Proof of Concept Grant:** Up to **₹20 Lakhs grant** for validation of proof of concept, prototype development, or software product trials.
  * **Commercialization Debt / Debentures:** Up to **₹50 Lakhs investment** for market launch and commercialization.
* **Relevance to a B.Tech CSE Graduate:**
  * If you build a web/mobile platform, SaaS product, or AI automation tool using your MERN stack or Java skills, you can register as a DPIIT-recognized startup (100% free online) and apply for seed funds through government-approved incubators (C-DAC, IITs, NITs).

## 8. CSIR Project Associate & Research Fellowship Scheme

* **Administering Body:** Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR).
* **Direct Official Portal:** [https://www.csir.res.in](https://www.csir.res.in)
* **What Financial Benefit It Gives You:**
  * **Project Associate - I:** Monthly consolidated stipend of **₹25,000 – ₹31,000/month + House Rent Allowance (HRA)**.
  * Work on high-level computational modeling, bio-informatics computing, network databases, or software development inside CSIR laboratories (e.g., CSIR-CRRI, CSIR-NPL, CSIR-CSIO).
* **Eligibility:** B.Tech in Computer Science / IT / Electronics with minimum 60% marks.
* **Selection:** Direct walk-in or online interview based on project vacancies published on individual CSIR lab websites.

## 9. National Career Service (NCS) Portal & Free Skill Assessment

* **Administering Body:** Ministry of Labour and Employment.
* **Direct Official Portal:** [https://www.ncs.gov.in](https://www.ncs.gov.in)
* **What Free Services It Gives You:**
  * Centralized Government Job Fair alerts and verified public/private IT vacancies.
  * Free Microsoft DigiSaksham certification in technical skills (Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, AI basics).
  * Official Career Skill Assessment reports recognizing your logical and programming abilities.
  * 100% free registration for all Indian graduates.
