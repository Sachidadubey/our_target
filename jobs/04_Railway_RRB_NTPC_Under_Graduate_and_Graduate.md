## 3. Benefits & Perks
* **Free First Class / 2nd AC Railway Passes:** Unlimited travel for self and dependent family across all Indian Railways express and superfast trains.
* **Medical Benefits (RELHS):** Complete cashless hospitalization across all Railway Divisional Hospitals.
* **Quarter Facilities:** Government quarters inside green, secure Railway Colonies.
* **Overtime & Running Allowance:** Substantial running allowance per kilometre for operating and train manager staff.
* **Leave & Pension:** 30 days LAP, 20 days LHAP, 8 CL, NPS retirement benefits.

---

## 4. Exact Eligibility & Age Limits
* **Under-Graduate Posts (CEN 07/2026):**
  * 12th (+2 Stage) OR its equivalent examination.
  * **Diploma Lateral Entry Candidates:** **100% Eligible!** Under AICTE & Ministry of Education Gazette, a 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma after 10th is legally equivalent to 10+2.
  * **Age Limit (As on 01 Jan 2026):** **18 to 33 Years** (Relaxation: OBC +3 yrs, SC/ST +5 yrs).
* **Graduate Posts (CEN 06/2026):**
  * Any Bachelor's Degree (B.Tech CSE fully accepted).
  * **Age Limit (As on 01 Jan 2026):** **18 to 36 Years** (Includes 3-year COVID age relaxation).

## 8. Selection Process & Exam Pattern

### Stage 1: Computer Based Test (CBT-1) — Screening (Common for All Posts)
* **Total Questions:** 100 | **Total Marks:** 100 | **Total Time:** **90 Minutes**.
* **Negative Marking:** 1/3rd (0.33) marks per wrong response.

| Section | Number of Questions | Maximum Marks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General Awareness** | 40 | 40 |
| **Mathematics** | 30 | 30 |
| **General Intelligence & Reasoning** | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** |

---

### Stage 2: Computer Based Test (CBT-2) — Merit Determiner
* **Total Questions:** 120 | **Total Marks:** 120 | **Total Time:** **90 Minutes**.
* **Sections:** General Awareness (50 Qs), Mathematics (35 Qs), General Intelligence & Reasoning (35 Qs).

### Subsequent Stages (Post-Dependent)
* **Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT):** Only for Station Master (Intelligence, Personality, Spatial scanning).
* **Typing Skill Test (TST):** For Junior Clerk, Accounts Clerk, Senior Clerk (30 wpm in English OR 25 wpm in Hindi).
* **Document Verification & Medical Examination.**

## 9. Complete Detailed Syllabus

### A. Mathematics (30 Qs in CBT-1 / 35 Qs in CBT-2)
* Number Systems, Decimals, Fractions, LCM & HCF, Ratio and Proportions, Percentage, Mensuration, Time and Work, Time and Distance, Simple and Compound Interest, Profit and Loss, Elementary Algebra, Geometry and Trigonometry, Elementary Statistics.

### B. General Intelligence & Reasoning (30 Qs in CBT-1 / 35 Qs in CBT-2)
* Analogies, Completion of Number and Alphabetical Series, Coding and Decoding, Mathematical Operations, Similarities and Differences, Relationships, Analytical Reasoning, Syllogism, Jumbling, Venn Diagrams, Puzzle, Data Sufficiency, Statement-Conclusion, Statement-Courses of Action, Decision Making, Maps, Interpretation of Graphs.

### C. General Awareness (40 Qs in CBT-1 / 50 Qs in CBT-2)
* Current Events of National and International Importance, Games and Sports, Art and Culture of India, Indian Literature, Monuments and Places of India, General Science and Life Science (up to 10th CBSE), History of India and Freedom Struggle, Physical, Social and Economic Geography of India and World, Indian Polity and Governance- constitution and political system, General Scientific and Technological Developments including Space and Nuclear Program of India, UN and Other important World Organizations, Environmental Issues Concerning India and World at Large, Basics of Computers and Computer Applications, Common Abbreviations, Transport Systems in India, Indian Economy, Famous Personalities of India and World, Flagship Government Programs.
