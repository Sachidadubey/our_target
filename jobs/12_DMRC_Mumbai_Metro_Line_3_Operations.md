# DMRC Mumbai Metro Line-3 Recruitment 2026: Junior Engineer (Systems/IT) & Train Operator

> **Recruiting Organization:** Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC) on behalf of Mumbai Metro Rail Corporation (MMRCL)  
> **Metro Line:** Mumbai Metro Line-3 (Aqua Line — Colaba-Bandra-SEEPZ Underground Corridor)  
> **Posts:** **Junior Engineer (Systems / IT / Signaling), Station Controller / Train Operator (SC/TO)**  
> **Candidate Eligibility:** 100% Eligible (**3-Year Polytechnic Diploma / B.Tech CSE / IT / Electronics**)

---

## 1. Organization & Post Profiles
* **Recruiting Body:** Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC) Operations & Maintenance Directorate.
* **Operating Line:** Mumbai Metro Line 3 — India's largest fully underground automated metro line (33.5 km corridor).
* **Posts:**
  1. **Junior Engineer (Systems / IT / Telecom / Signaling):** Maintain Automated Train Operation (ATO), Communication-Based Train Control (CBTC), SCADA systems, fiber-optic backbone, and passenger information servers.
  2. **Station Controller / Train Operator (SC/TO):** Station management, emergency train operations, and customer transit flow.

* **Medical Fitness Standard:** **A-1 Category** for Train Operator (Distance Vision 6/6 without glasses); **A-3 / B-1 Category** for Junior Engineer (Glasses permitted up to -4D).

## 8. Selection Process & Exam Pattern

### Stage 1: Computer Based Test (CBT)
* Conducted in a single session with two distinct papers:

#### Paper-I: Technical Aptitude & General Awareness
* **Questions:** 120 Questions | **Time:** 90 Minutes.
  * Technical Subject (Computer Science / Electronics): 75 Qs
  * Quantitative Aptitude & Logical Reasoning: 30 Qs
  * General Awareness: 15 Qs
* **Negative Marking:** 1/3rd (0.33) marks deducted per wrong answer.

#### Paper-II: General English Language
* **Questions:** 60 Questions | **Time:** 45 Minutes.
* Tests comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, and sentence rearrangement.

### Stage 2: Psycho Aptitude Test (Only for Train Operators)
* Memory test, spatial scanning, following directions, reaction time, and vigilance test.

### Stage 3: Medical Examination at Railway / Metro Empanelled Hospital.

## 9. Complete Detailed Syllabus (Junior Engineer IT / Systems)
* **Computer Networks & Communication:** OSI reference model, TCP/IP, IP addressing (IPv4/IPv6), Fiber Optic Communications, Optical Time Domain Reflectometer (OTDR), Ethernet switches, Routers, VLANs.
* **Operating Systems & Linux:** Linux fundamentals, shell scripting, Process management, Network configuration commands (`ifconfig`, `netstat`, `ping`, `traceroute`).
* **Microprocessors & Digital Electronics:** Logic gates, Multiplexers, Counters, Registers, Microcontroller basics (8051 / ARM architecture).
* **Database & SCADA Systems:** Basics of Relational Databases, SQL queries, SCADA architecture, Programmable Logic Controllers (PLC), Sensor interfaces.
* **Aptitude & English:** Speed math, Quadratic equations, Syllogisms, Reading comprehension, Active/Passive voice, Error spotting.
