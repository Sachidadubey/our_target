# Supreme Court of India: Junior Court Assistant (JCA) Recruitment 2026

> **Organization:** Supreme Court of India (Apex Court of the Republic of India), New Delhi  
> **Post:** **Junior Court Assistant (Group 'B' Non-Gazetted Post)**  
> **Candidate Eligibility:** 100% Eligible (Any Bachelor's Degree / B.Tech CSE / Lateral Entry)  
> **Salary:** Pay Matrix Level 6 (**~₹68,000 – ₹74,000/month Gross**)

---

## 1. Organization & Post Profiles
* **Recruiting Body:** Registry of the Supreme Court of India, Tilak Marg, New Delhi.
* **Role:** Manage court case filing registries, digital listing of petitions, legal drafting records, assisting Registrar benches, and judicial digital record management.
* **Work Location:** Exclusively at the Supreme Court of India Complex, New Delhi. Prestigious work environment with elite administrative exposure.

## 8. Selection Process & Exam Pattern
Selection consists of a 4-tier evaluation conducted over 2 stages:

| Component | Questions / Format | Maximum Marks | Duration | Negative Marking |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Objective Written Test** | 100 Questions (English 50, Aptitude 25, GA 25) | 100 Marks | 2 Hours (combined) | 0.25 marks |
| **Computer Knowledge Test** | 25 Questions (Objective) | 25 Marks | Included in 2 Hours | Nil |
| **Typing Test on Computer** | 35 wpm speed test | Qualifying (Max 3% mistakes allowed) | 10 Minutes | Mistakes penalized |
| **Descriptive English Test** | Comprehension, Precis & Essay writing | 50 Marks | 2 Hours | Nil |
| **Interview** | Personal Interview before Supreme Court Officers | 25 Marks | ~15 Minutes | Nil |

---

## 9. Complete Detailed Syllabus

### A. Objective Written Test (100 Marks)
* **General English (50 Marks — 50% Weightage):** Comprehension passages, Spotting errors, Synonyms & Antonyms, Sentence improvement, Idioms and phrases, Active and passive voice, Direct and indirect speech, One-word substitution.
* **General Aptitude (25 Marks):** Reasoning puzzles, Syllogisms, Number series, Coding-decoding, Blood relations, Direction test, Basic arithmetic (Percentage, Average, Ratio, Profit & Loss).
* **General Awareness (25 Marks):** Indian Constitution & Judicial System (Supreme Court, High Courts, Fundamental Rights, Articles), Current Affairs (National & International), History & Geography, Science basics.

### B. Computer Knowledge Test (25 Marks)
* Computer Hardware basics, Windows Operating System, MS Word & MS Excel shortcuts, Internet protocols, Email drafting, Cybersecurity basics.

### C. Descriptive English Test (50 Marks)
* **Essay Writing (20 Marks):** Topics on Legal Reforms, Digital India, Environmental Conservation, Women Empowerment, Artificial Intelligence.
* **Precis Writing (15 Marks):** Condensing a legal or analytical passage to one-third of its length with a suitable title.
* **Comprehension Questions (15 Marks):** Answering direct and interpretive questions from an advanced passage.
