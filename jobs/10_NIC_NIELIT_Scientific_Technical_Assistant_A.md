# NIC / NIELIT: Scientific & Technical Assistant 'A' & Scientist 'B' (MeitY)

> **Recruiting Body:** National Informatics Centre (NIC) conducted by NIELIT (Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology)  
> **Posts:** **Scientific/Technical Assistant 'A' (Level 6) & Scientist 'B' (Level 10)**  
> **Candidate Eligibility:** 100% Eligible (B.E. / B.Tech Computer Science / IT / 3-Year Diploma Lateral Entry)  
> **Selection:** 100% Written Exam Based for Assistant 'A' (**ZERO INTERVIEW**)

---

## 1. Organization & Post Profiles
* **Recruiting Organization:** National Informatics Centre (NIC), Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India.
* **Role:** NIC is the premier technology organization of the Government of India, driving Digital India platforms (Aadhaar authentication services, DigiLocker, UPI backend, CoWIN, e-Courts, GST portal, National Government Cloud).
* **Posts:**
  1. **Scientific / Technical Assistant 'A':** Group 'B' Non-Gazetted Central Government Technical Cadre.
  2. **Scientist 'B':** Group 'A' Gazetted Central Government Officer.

## 9. Complete Detailed Syllabus (Technical CS/IT — 65%)

### Technical Core:
1. **Computer Systems & Architecture:** Number representations, CPU design, Pipelining, Cache memory, Virtual memory.
2. **Operating Systems:** Process management, Threads, CPU scheduling, Memory management, Paging, Segmentation, File systems, Deadlock handling.
3. **Databases (DBMS):** E-R diagrams, Relational model, SQL (Queries, Joins, Triggers), Normalization (1NF to BCNF), Transaction management, Concurrency control.
4. **Computer Networks:** OSI 7 Layers, TCP/IP, IP addressing (IPv4, IPv6), Subnetting, Routing protocols, Transport layer protocols (TCP, UDP), Application layer (HTTP, DNS, SSL/TLS), Network security.
5. **Data Structures & Algorithms:** Arrays, Stacks, Queues, Linked Lists, Trees, Binary Search Trees, Heaps, Graphs, Sorting algorithms, Searching, Big-O complexity analysis.
6. **Web Programming & Software Engineering:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Client-Server architecture, SDLC phases, Agile methodologies, Testing fundamentals.

### Section 'A': Generic Aptitude (35%)
* Logical Reasoning, Analytical Ability, Quantitative Aptitude (Number series, Ratio, Percentage, Time & Work), General English comprehension.
