## 3. Benefits & Perks
* **Gazetted Executive Feeder Post:** Sub-Engineers supervise municipal contracts, engineering projects, and IT systems. Eligible for promotion to Assistant Engineer (AE - Class II Officer) in 5–7 years.
* **Government Quarters:** Allotment in PWD / WRD residential engineering quarters across district headquarters.
* **Fixed Local Posting:** Complete posting stability within the state of Madhya Pradesh.
* **Field & Vehicle Inspection Allowances.**
* **Leave & Pension:** MP State Civil Pension (NPS), GPF, 30 days Earned Leave, medical reimbursement.

## 4. Exact Eligibility & Age Limits
* **Educational Qualification:**
  * **3-Year Polytechnic Diploma in Engineering** (Computer Science, Information Technology, Civil, Electrical, Mechanical) from a recognized Board/University.
  * **B.E. / B.Tech Candidates:** **Fully Eligible!** Under MP Civil Services Recruitment Rules, higher qualification (B.Tech degree in the relevant discipline) is valid and recognized alongside Diploma.
  * **Your Profile (Diploma CSE + B.Tech CSE Lateral Entry):** **Double Qualified!** You possess both the exact primary qualification (3-Year Polytechnic Diploma) and the advanced qualification (B.Tech).
* **Age Limit (As on 01 January 2026):**
  * **18 to 40 Years** (For Male General / EWS candidates).
  * **18 to 45 Years** (For Female candidates, and SC / ST / OBC / Govt employees of MP).
  * *Other State candidates are eligible under General category (Age up to 40 years).*

## 9. Complete Detailed Syllabus

### Part 'A': General Subjects (100 Marks)
* **General Science (14m):** Class 10 NCERT Physics, Chemistry, Biology (Motion, Electricity, Optics, Periodic Table, Acids, Human Body).
* **General Mathematics (14m):** Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Average, Profit & Loss, Simple/Compound Interest, Time & Work, Speed & Distance, Simplification.
* **Reasoning (15m):** Coding-decoding, Series, Blood relations, Analogies, Direction sense, Venn diagrams, Puzzles.
* **Computer Knowledge (15m — Free marks for CSE!):** OS, CPU, RAM/ROM, Networking basics, MS Office, Shortcut keys, Security.
* **General Hindi (14m):** Sandhi, Samas, Muhavare, Vilom, Paryayvachi, Vartani Shuddhi.
* **General English (14m):** Tenses, Prepositions, Spotting errors, Synonyms/Antonyms, One-word substitutions.
* **General Knowledge (14m):** MP State GK (Rivers, Dams, National Parks, Monuments, Forts), Current Affairs, Indian Polity.

### Part 'B': Technical Engineering (Computer Science — 100 Marks)
* **Digital Electronics & Computer Architecture:** Logic gates, Boolean algebra, Combinational circuits, CPU, Instruction cycle, Pipelining, Memory hierarchy.
* **Data Structures & Algorithms:** Arrays, Stacks, Queues, Linked Lists, Trees, Graph traversals (BFS, DFS), Sorting (Quick, Merge, Heap), Searching.
* **Operating Systems:** Process scheduling, Deadlocks, Memory management, Paging, Virtual memory, Linux/Unix shell commands.
* **Database Management Systems (DBMS):** Relational algebra, SQL commands (DDL, DML, DQL), Normalization (1NF, 2NF, 3NF), Transactions, ACID.
* **Computer Networks:** OSI layers, TCP/IP, IP addressing, Subnetting, Routing algorithms, Application layer protocols (HTTP, DNS, SMTP).
* **Programming Languages & Web:** C, C++, Core Java, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Web architectures.
