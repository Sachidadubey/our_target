## 1. Organization & Post Profiles
* **Recruiting Body:** Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB), Ministry of Railways, Government of India.
* **Zones Participating:** All 21 RRBs (RRB Allahabad, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chandigarh, Chennai, Secunderabad, Bhopal, Patna, etc.).
* **Applied Posts:**
  1. **Junior Engineer (Information Technology):** Server administration, railway PRS (Passenger Reservation System), FOIS (Freight Operations), railway cybersecurity, and networking.
  2. **Junior Engineer (Track Machine / Works / Electronics):** Railway automated infrastructure & electronics signaling.

## 2. Salary Structure & Allowances
* **Pay Matrix Level:** Level 6 (7th Central Pay Commission).
* **Basic Pay:** ₹35,400/month.
* **Dearness Allowance (DA):** 50% of Basic = ₹17,700/month.
* **House Rent Allowance (HRA):**
  * X Cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata): 30% = ₹10,620/month.
  * Y Cities (Patna, Bhopal, Lucknow): 20% = ₹7,080/month.
  * Z Cities (Small towns): 10% = ₹3,540/month.
  * *(Or free Railway Quarters inside Railway Colonies).*
* **Transport Allowance (TPTA):** ₹3,600 + DA = ₹5,400/month.
* **Railway Special Technical Allowance:** ₹2,000 – ₹4,000/month.
* **Gross Monthly Salary:** **~₹62,000 – ₹73,000/month**.
* **In-Hand Starting Salary:** **~₹55,000 – ₹64,000/month**.

## 9. Complete Detailed Syllabus

### A. CBT-1 Mathematics (30 Marks)
* Number Systems, BODMAS, Decimals, Fractions, LCM and HCF, Ratio and Proportion, Percentages, Mensuration (Area & Volume), Time and Work, Time and Distance, Simple and Compound Interest, Profit and Loss, Algebra (Linear & Quadratic equations), Geometry (Triangles, Circles), Trigonometry (Heights & Distances), Elementary Statistics.

### B. CBT-1 General Science (30 Marks — Scoring Game Changer!)
* **Physics (10-12 Qs):** Units and Dimensions, Motion, Force and Laws of Motion, Gravitation, Work, Energy & Power, Sound, Light (Reflection, Refraction, Lenses, Mirrors), Electricity (Ohm's law, Resistance in Series/Parallel, Electric Power), Magnetic effects of electric current.
* **Chemistry (10-12 Qs):** Matter in our surroundings, Atoms and Molecules, Structure of the Atom, Chemical Reactions and Equations, Acids, Bases and Salts, Metals and Non-Metals, Carbon and its Compounds, Periodic Classification of Elements.
* **Life Sciences (6-8 Qs):** Life Processes (Nutrition, Respiration, Circulation, Excretion), Control and Coordination, Cell Organelles, Reproduction, Heredity, Our Environment, Natural Resources.

### C. CBT-2 Technical Abilities: Computer Science & IT (100 Marks)
* **DBMS:** Relational Model, ER Diagrams, Normalization (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF), SQL Queries, Transactions & Concurrency.
* **Operating Systems:** Process Management, CPU Scheduling, Deadlocks, Memory Management (Paging, Virtual Memory), File Systems.
* **Computer Networks:** OSI 7 Layers, TCP/IP, IP Addressing (Subnetting, CIDR), Routing Protocols, DNS, HTTP, Cryptography.
* **Data Structures & Algorithms:** Arrays, Linked Lists, Stacks, Queues, Binary Trees, Graphs, Sorting & Searching, Time Complexities.
* **Web Tech & Programming:** C/C++, Java, HTML/CSS, JavaScript fundamentals, Software Engineering models (SDLC, Agile).
