## 1. Organization & Post Profiles
* **Recruiting Body:** Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS).
* **Participating Banks:** 43 Regional Rural Banks across India (e.g., Aryavart Bank, Baroda UP Bank, Prathama UP Gramin Bank, Dakshin Bihar Gramin Bank, Madhya Pradesh Gramin Bank, Madhyanchal Gramin Bank, Punjab Gramin Bank).
* **Applied Posts:**

## 3. Benefits & Perks
* **Home State Postings:** RRBs operate within specific districts/states. Unlike commercial PSBs, transfers are strictly limited to your local state or zone.
* **Definite Working Hours:** Fixed banking hours (10:00 AM – 5:00 PM). Minimal weekend pressure.
* **Medical Benefits:** Full annual medical aid reimbursement + Group Health Insurance for employee and family.
* **Leave Entitlements:** 30 days Privilege Leave (PL), 12 Casual Leaves (CL), 15 Sick Leaves, Maternity/Paternity leave.
* **Retirement Security:** National Pension System (NPS) with 14% Government/Bank contribution, Gratuity, and Leave Encashment.
* **Festival Advance & Concessional Staff Loans:** Home loans and vehicle loans at 4%–5% concessional interest rates.

---

## 4. Exact Eligibility & Age Limits
* **Nationality:** Citizen of India.
* **Educational Qualification:**
  * **Office Assistant:** Bachelor’s degree in ANY discipline from a recognized University. Proficiency in the local language of the State/UT is required. Working knowledge of Computer Systems (B.Tech CSE degree automatically satisfies this).
  * **Officer Scale-I:** Bachelor’s degree in ANY discipline. Preference is given to candidates with degrees in Computer Science/IT, Agriculture, Management, and Law.
  * **Diploma Lateral Entry B.Tech:** **100% Eligible!** AICTE recognized 3-Year B.Tech degree is fully valid. No 12th certificate required.
* **Age Limit (As on 01 June 2026):**
  * Office Assistant: **18 to 28 Years** (Born between 02 June 1998 and 01 June 2008).
  * Officer Scale-I: **18 to 30 Years** (Born between 03 June 1996 and 30 June 2008).
  * *Age Relaxation:* OBC: +3 Years; SC/ST: +5 Years; PwBD: +10 Years.

## 8. Selection Process & Exam Pattern

### Stage 1: Preliminary Examination (Crucial Secret: NO ENGLISH SECTION!)
* **Total Questions:** 80
* **Total Marks:** 80
* **Composite Time:** **45 Minutes** (No sectional timer! Complete freedom to jump between sections).
* **Negative Marking:** 0.25 (1/4th) marks deducted per wrong answer.

| Section | Number of Questions | Maximum Marks | Medium of Exam | Recommended Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reasoning Ability** | 40 | 40 | Regional Language / English / Hindi | 22 Minutes |
| **Quantitative Aptitude** | 40 | 40 | Regional Language / English / Hindi | 23 Minutes |
| **Total** | **80** | **80** | | **45 Minutes** |

---

### Stage 2: Main Examination (For Final Merit / Selection)
* **Total Questions:** 200 | **Total Marks:** 200 | **Total Time:** 2 Hours (120 Minutes).
* **Sections:**
  1. Reasoning: 40 Qs (50 Marks)
  2. Computer Knowledge: 40 Qs (20 Marks) — *100% scoring for CSE graduates!*
  3. General Awareness: 40 Qs (40 Marks) — Current affairs & banking.
  4. English Language OR Hindi Language: 40 Qs (40 Marks) — *Candidate can choose Hindi!*
  5. Quantitative Aptitude: 40 Qs (50 Marks)

### Stage 3: Interview (Officer Scale-I Only)
* Personal Interview carries 100 marks. Minimum qualifying mark is 40% (35% for SC/ST/OBC).
* Final merit weightage: **80% (Mains Exam) : 20% (Interview)**.
* *For Office Assistant:* **Zero Interview!** 100% selection based on Mains exam score.

## 9. Complete Detailed Syllabus

### A. Quantitative Aptitude (Prelims + Mains)
* **Speed Math:** Simplification & Approximation (BODMAS, Fractions, Decimals, Surds), Missing Number Series, Wrong Number Series, Quadratic Equations ($ax^2 + bx + c = 0$).
* **Data Interpretation:** Tabular DI, Bar Graph, Line Graph, Pie Chart, Caselet DI, Missing DI.
* **Core Arithmetic:** Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Averages, Profit, Loss & Discount, Simple & Compound Interest, Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns, Speed, Time & Distance, Trains, Boats & Streams, Mixtures & Alligations, Mensuration (2D & 3D).

### B. Reasoning Ability (Prelims + Mains)
* **High-Yield Scoring:** Syllogisms ("Only a few", "Some", "All", "No"), Inequalities ($>, <, \ge, \le, =$), Coding-Decoding (Substitution / Chinese), Direction Sense, Blood Relations, Alpha-Numeric-Symbol Series, Order & Ranking.
* **Puzzles & Seating:** Circular Seating (Facing Inside/Outside), Linear Arrangement (Single/Parallel Row), Floor & Flat Puzzles, Box Puzzles, Month & Date Puzzles, Scheduling.

### C. Computer Knowledge (Mains — 20 Marks)
* Computer Hardware, OS Basics, Networking (OSI Model, IP, Protocols), Database Concepts (DBMS, SQL, Primary Key), Computer Security & Viruses, MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint shortcuts), Internet & Cloud Basics, Number Systems (Binary, Hexadecimal).
