## 1. Organization & Post Profiles
* **Recruiting Body:** Staff Selection Commission (SSC), New Delhi.
* **Top Central Cadre Posts:**
  1. **Assistant Section Officer (ASO):** Ministry of External Affairs (MEA - foreign postings in Indian embassies), Central Secretariat Service (CSS - North/South Block, Prime Minister's Office, Home Ministry), Intelligence Bureau (IB).
  2. **Inspectors:** Inspector of Income Tax (CBDT), Preventive Officer / Examiner / GST & Central Excise Inspector (CBIC).
  3. **Investigative Cadre:** Sub-Inspector in Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) & National Investigation Agency (NIA), Assistant Enforcement Officer (ED).
  4. **CHSL Technical Posts:** Data Entry Operator (DEO Grade 'A') in Controller General of Accounts (CGA) and Lower Division Clerk (LDC).

## 2. Salary Structure & Allowances

### Level 7 Posts (ASO, Income Tax Inspector, GST Inspector, CBI SI)
* **Pay Scale:** Level 7 (₹44,900 – ₹1,42,400).
* **Basic Pay:** ₹44,900/month.
* **Dearness Allowance (DA):** 50% = ₹22,450/month.
* **House Rent Allowance (HRA):** 30% ('X' Cities — Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata) = ₹13,470/month.
* **Transport Allowance (TPTA):** ₹3,600 + DA = ₹5,400/month.
* **Special Security Allowance (For CBI / IB / ED):** 20% of Basic = ₹8,980/month.
* **Gross Starting Monthly Salary:** **~₹86,220/month** (In Delhi/Metro); **~₹95,000/month in CBI/IB**.
* **In-Hand Starting Salary:** **~₹76,000 – ₹83,000/month**.

### Level 4 Posts (CHSL Data Entry Operator Grade 'A')
* **Basic Pay:** ₹25,500/month.
* **Gross Starting Salary:** **~₹48,000 – ₹52,000/month**.

### Level 2 Posts (CHSL Lower Division Clerk / JSA)
* **Basic Pay:** ₹19,900/month.
* **Gross Starting Salary:** **~₹38,000 – ₹42,000/month**.

## 3. Benefits & Perks
* **Elite Diplomatic / Secretariat Exposure:** ASOs in MEA represent India abroad with foreign living allowance (payable in US Dollars, €3,000 – $4,500/month tax-free during overseas embassy postings).
* **Government Accommodation:** Central Government GPRA Quarters (Type-IV / Type-V flats in central Delhi locations like Chanakyapuri, Lodhi Road, Vasant Kunj).
* **CGHS Healthcare:** Cashless medical care across India.
* **Stable 5-Day Work Week:** Fixed 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM working hours, Saturday & Sunday strictly off.
* **Leave & Pension:** 30 days Earned Leave, 8 Casual Leaves, 20 Half-Pay Leaves, Child care leave, NPS retirement scheme.

* **Educational Qualification:**
  * **SSC CGL:** Bachelor’s Degree in ANY discipline from a recognized University.
  * **SSC CHSL:** 12th Standard OR **3-Year Polytechnic Diploma in Engineering** (legally equivalent under AICTE Gazette notification).
  * **Diploma Lateral Entry B.Tech:** **100% Eligible!**
* **Age Limits (As on 01 August 2026):**
  * **ASO & Inspectors:** **18 to 30 Years** (Born between 02 August 1996 and 01 August 2008).
  * **Junior Statistical Officer (JSO):** Up to 32 Years.
  * **CHSL (LDC / DEO):** **18 to 27 Years**.
  * *Relaxation:* OBC: +3 Years; SC/ST: +5 Years; PwBD: +10 Years.

## 8. Selection Process & Exam Pattern (SSC CGL)

### Tier-I: Preliminary Examination (CBT — Qualifying Only)
* **Total Questions:** 100 | **Total Marks:** 200 | **Time:** **60 Minutes**.
* **Negative Marking:** 0.50 marks per wrong answer.
* *Subjects:* Reasoning (25 Qs / 50m), General Awareness (25 Qs / 50m), Quantitative Aptitude (25 Qs / 50m), English Comprehension (25 Qs / 50m).

---

## 9. Complete Detailed Syllabus (Tier-II)

### A. Mathematical Abilities (30 Qs — 90 Marks)
* Number Systems, Fundamental Arithmetical Operations (Percentages, Ratio, Square roots, Averages, Interest, Profit and Loss, Discount, Partnership, Mixture and Alligation, Time and distance, Time and work), Algebra (Basic algebraic identities, Elementary surds, Linear equations), Geometry (Triangles and its chords, tangents, angles subtended), Mensuration, Trigonometry (Trigonometric ratios, Heights and Distances), Statistics & Probability (Mean, Median, Mode, Standard deviation, Simple probabilities).

### B. Reasoning & General Intelligence (30 Qs — 90 Marks)
* Semantic Analogy, Symbolic/Number Analogy, Figural Analogy, Semantic Classification, Venn Diagrams, Critical thinking, Emotional Intelligence, Social Intelligence, Coding and de-coding, Numerical operations.

### C. English Language & Comprehension (45 Qs — 135 Marks — Highest Weightage!)
* Vocabulary, Grammar, Sentence structure, Synonyms, Antonyms and their correct usage, Spot the Error, Fill in the Blanks, Idioms & Phrases, One word substitution, Improvement of Sentences, Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect narration, Shuffling of Sentence parts, Cloze Passage, Comprehension Passages.

### D. Computer Knowledge Module (20 Qs — 60 Marks — Effortless for CSE!)
* CPU, RAM, ROM, Input/Output devices, Memory organization, Windows Operating System basics, MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint, Web Browsing & Searching, Emails, Transferring files, Networking protocols (HTTP, FTP, IP, TCP/UDP), Cyber Security, Malware, Worms, Trojans, Ransomware.
