## 9. Complete Detailed Syllabus

### A. English Language (Prelims: 30 Marks)
* Reading Comprehension (Story/Economy-based with 2 vocab questions), Cloze Test (Fill in the blanks in paragraph), Spotting Errors (Grammar: Subject-Verb, Tenses, Prepositions), Sentence Rearrangement / Para Jumbles, Fillers (Single/Double), Misspelt words.

### B. Numerical Ability (Prelims: 35 Marks)
* **Speed Math:** Simplification & Approximation (10–15 Qs), Missing & Wrong Number Series (5 Qs), Quadratic Equations (5 Qs).
* **Data Interpretation:** 1 Tabular or Bar Graph Set (5 Qs).
* **Arithmetic Word Problems:** Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Averages, Profit & Loss, Simple/Compound Interest, Time & Work, Speed, Time & Distance (7–10 Qs).

### C. Reasoning Ability (Prelims: 35 Marks)
* **Speed Scoring:** Syllogisms (5 Qs), Inequalities (5 Qs), Alpha-Numeric-Symbol Series (5 Qs), Coding-Decoding (5 Qs), Direction Sense & Blood Relations (3–5 Qs).
* **Puzzles & Seating:** 2 to 3 Basic Puzzles (10–15 Qs) — Circular facing inside, 7-person linear, Box puzzle.
