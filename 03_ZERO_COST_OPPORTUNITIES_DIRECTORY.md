## 2. Zero-Cost Level 10 Gazetted Officer Entries

### 2.1 Indian Army TGC-145 (Permanent Commission)
* **Post:** Lieutenant (Permanent Commission in the Indian Army, Technical/Signals).
* **Cost to Apply:** **₹0 (Completely Free)**.
* **Travel Allowance (TA):** If appearing for your first SSB interview, the Indian Army reimburses the full **AC 3-Tier train fare (or bus fare)** from your home station to the Selection Centre and back.
* **Monthly Salary & Perks:**
  * Pay Level 10: Basic ₹56,100 + Military Service Pay (MSP) ₹15,500 + DA (50%) ₹35,800.
  * **Starting Gross: ~₹1,15,000/month**.
  * Free officer housing, full military healthcare, CSD canteen, subsidized rations, 60 days annual leave.
* **Eligibility:** B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering / IT / Computer Technology. Final year 2026 students or fresh graduates. Age: 20 to 27 years. Unmarried male.
* **Selection Process:** **No written test**. Direct shortlisting based on B.Tech marks $\rightarrow$ 5-Day SSB Interview (Screening, Psychology, GTO, Personal Interview) $\rightarrow$ Medical Board.
* **Application Window:** **30 September 2026 to 29 October 2026 (03:00 PM)**.
* **Direct Apply Link:** [https://joinindianarmy.nic.in/login.htm](https://joinindianarmy.nic.in/login.htm)
* **Documents Needed:** Class 10th certificate, 12th marksheet, B.Tech semester marksheets, CGPA conversion rule sheet, photo (20–50 KB), signature (10–20 KB).

---

### 2.2 Indian Army SSC Tech (Men)
* **Post:** Lieutenant (Short Service Commission - 10 to 14 Years, with Permanent Commission option).
* **Cost to Apply:** **₹0 (Completely Free)**. Free 3rd AC travel reimbursement for first SSB.
* **Monthly Salary:** Same as TGC: Level 10 (Gross: **~₹1,15,000/month**).
* **Eligibility:** B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering / IT. Age: 20 to 27 years.
* **Selection Process:** Direct shortlisting on B.Tech aggregate $\rightarrow$ 5-Day SSB $\rightarrow$ Medicals.
* **Cycle:** Releases twice a year (Next notification in **December 2026 / January 2027** for October 2027 batch at Officers Training Academy, Chennai).
* **Direct Portal:** [https://joinindianarmy.nic.in](https://joinindianarmy.nic.in)

---

### 2.3 Indian Navy SSC Executive (Information Technology)
* **Post:** Sub Lieutenant (Executive Branch - Information Technology).
* **Cost to Apply:** **₹0 (Completely Free)**. Free 3rd AC travel reimbursement for SSB.
* **Monthly Salary & Perks:**
  * Pay Level 10: Basic ₹56,100 + MSP ₹15,500 + DA.
  * **Starting Gross: ~₹1,15,000/month** + Sea-duty allowance when deployed on ships.
  * Free accommodation in naval base, mess membership, naval hospital healthcare.
* **Eligibility:** B.Tech in CSE / IT with minimum 60% marks + minimum 60% marks in English in Class 10 or 12. Age: 19.5 to 25 years.
* **Selection Process:** **No written examination**. Shortlisting based on B.Tech percentage $\rightarrow$ 5-Day SSB Interview at Naval Selection Boards (Bhopal/Visakhapatnam/Bengaluru) $\rightarrow$ Naval Medicals.
* **Expected Cycle:** July 2027 Course notification expected **mid-November 2026**.
* **Direct Apply Link:** [https://www.joinindiannavy.gov.in](https://www.joinindiannavy.gov.in)
* **Documents Needed:** 10th & 12th marksheets, B.Tech semester marksheets, CGPA formula document, photo, signature.

## 6. Zero-Cost Official Preparation Portals & PYQ Repositories

Avoid buying expensive coaching courses or test series. The Government of India and exam commissions provide official question papers, answer keys, and mock test simulators **100% free of charge**:

### 6.1 Official Mock Test Engines & Question Paper Vaults
* **UPSC Official Previous Year Papers & Answer Keys:**
  * 100% free download of all Civil Services Prelims GS Paper I and CSAT Paper II papers from 2014 to 2026 with authoritative answer keys.
  * *Link:* [https://upsc.gov.in/examinations/previous-question-papers](https://upsc.gov.in/examinations/previous-question-papers)
* **SSC Official Candidate Practice Engine & Answer Keys:**
  * Free CBT practice interface replicating the exact SSC exam software.
  * *Link:* [https://ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in)
* **RRB Official Mock Test Simulator:**
  * Official Railway Recruitment Board mock test links released 10 days before exams to practice screen navigation, language toggling, and timer pacing.
  * *Link:* [https://www.rrbapply.gov.in](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in)
* **ISRO ICRB Previous Question Papers with Answer Keys:**
  * Complete official repository of all previous Scientist/Engineer 'SC' (Computer Science) question papers from 2013 to 2026 with official answer keys.
  * *Link:* [https://www.isro.gov.in/PreviousYearsQuestionPapers.html](https://www.isro.gov.in/PreviousYearsQuestionPapers.html)
* **DRDO RAC Scientist 'B' Interview Insights & Question Bank:**
  * Official guidelines and syllabus breakdowns for Computer Science interview boards.
  * *Link:* [https://rac.gov.in](https://rac.gov.in)
