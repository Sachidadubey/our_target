## 🏛️ 2. EXACT JOB-BY-JOB ELIGIBILITY BREAKDOWN (DIPLOMA + B.TECH)

---

### 1. Indian Army Technical Graduate Course (TGC-145) — ✅ 100% ELIGIBLE
* **Official Army Rule on Lateral Entry:** The Indian Army recognizes the B.Tech degree as the qualifying educational criteria regardless of whether you joined in the 1st year (after 12th) or 2nd year (after Diploma).
* **How to Fill the Form on [joinindianarmy.nic.in](https://joinindianarmy.nic.in):**
  * In the education section, select **"Diploma in Engineering"** under the qualifying educational stream prior to B.Tech.
  * Enter your Diploma marks.
  * For B.Tech semesters, enter marks from **Semester 3 to Semester 8** (since Semester 1 & 2 are exempted for lateral entry students).
  * Your aggregate percentage is calculated on the semesters you actually studied.
* **Perks & Pay:** Commissioned as **Lieutenant (Level 10 Gazetted: ~₹1,15,000/month)**. **₹0 application fee.**

---

### 2. Indian Navy SSC Executive (Information Technology) — ✅ 100% ELIGIBLE
* **Official Navy Rule:** The Indian Navy notification states:
  > *"Candidates must have scored minimum 60% marks in English in Class 10th OR Class 12th AND minimum 60% in B.E./B.Tech."*
* **Why You Qualify:** You have a Class 10th certificate! As long as you scored $\ge$ 60% in English in your 10th Board exam, you meet the English criteria.
* **How to Fill the Form on [joinindiannavy.gov.in](https://www.joinindiannavy.gov.in):**
  * Select Education Type: **"Diploma $\rightarrow$ Engineering Graduate (Lateral Entry)"**.
* **Perks & Pay:** Sub Lieutenant (Level 10: **~₹1,15,000/month**). **₹0 application fee.**

---

### 3. Railway Recruitment Boards — RRB JE (CEN 04/2026) — ✅ DOUBLE ADVANTAGE!
* **Why You Have an Unfair Advantage:**
  * RRB Junior Engineer accepts **EITHER** a 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma **OR** a B.Tech in Engineering.
  * You possess **BOTH**! You meet the minimum qualification via your Diploma and the higher qualification via your B.Tech.
* **Pay & Post:** Level 6 Junior Engineer (Information Technology / Electronics). **Gross: ~₹58,000 – ₹66,000/month**.

---

### 4. Railway Recruitment Boards — RRB NTPC Graduate Level (CEN 06/2026) — ✅ 100% ELIGIBLE
* **Official Railway Rule:** Requires a *"Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized University"*.
* **Status:** Your Lateral Entry B.Tech is an AICTE/UGC recognized Bachelor's Degree. 12th is not required.
* **Pay & Post:** Station Master (Level 6: **~₹62,000/mo**), Goods Train Manager (Level 5: **~₹55,000/mo**).
* **Application Dates:** **Opens 08 October 2026**.

---

### 5. ISRO Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB) — Scientist 'SC' (CS) — ✅ 100% ELIGIBLE
* **Official ISRO FAQ Clause:**
  > *"Candidates who have pursued Diploma + B.E./B.Tech (Lateral Entry) are eligible, provided they have secured First Class with an aggregate minimum of 65% marks or 6.84/10 CGPA in B.E./B.Tech."*
* **Condition:** 12th marksheet is **never asked**. Only your B.Tech aggregate (minimum 65%) and Class 10th (DOB proof) are verified.
* **Pay & Post:** Level 10 Space Scientist (**Gross: ~₹95,000 – ₹1,05,000/month**).

### 6. DRDO RAC — Scientist 'B' (Computer Science & Engineering) — ✅ 100% ELIGIBLE

* **Pay & Post:** Level 10 Defence Scientist (**Gross: ~₹1,00,000/month**).

### 7. BARC (Bhabha Atomic Research Centre) — Scientific Officer 'C' — ✅ 100% ELIGIBLE

### 8. SEBI Grade A (Assistant Manager — IT Stream) — ✅ 100% ELIGIBLE

## ❌ 3. THE ONLY 2 EXAMINATIONS WHERE YOU ARE INELIGIBLE

Because you do not have a 10+2 / 12th standard certificate with Physics & Mathematics, you are restricted **ONLY** from the following two military aviation recruitments:

### 1. Indian Air Force AFCAT (Air Force Common Admission Test)
* **The Ineligibility Clause:**
  > *"Candidates must have mandatory minimum 50% marks each in Physics and Mathematics at 10+2 level AND minimum 60% in B.E./B.Tech."*
* **Why Blocked:** The Air Force Flying and Aeronautical Engineering branches strictly verify CBSE/State Board 12th Physics & Math marksheets. A 3-year polytechnic diploma is not accepted as a substitute for 10+2 Physics/Math in AFCAT.

### 2. Indian Coast Guard (Assistant Commandant - Technical)
* **The Ineligibility Clause:**
  > *"Must have passed 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics with minimum 55% aggregate marks."*
* **Why Blocked:** Coast Guard technical branch adheres to the strict 10+2 school board requirement.

> [!NOTE]
> **Summary:** You are barred from **AFCAT and Coast Guard**. You are **100% ELIGIBLE** for the **Indian Army (TGC/SSC Tech)**, **Indian Navy (SSC IT)**, **ISRO**, **DRDO**, **BARC**, **Railways**, **Banking**, **PSUs**, **SEBI**, **SSC**, and **UPSC**.

## 📝 4. HOW TO FILL ONLINE FORMS AS A DIPLOMA LATERAL ENTRY STUDENT

When filling government job application portals, follow these exact guidelines:

1. **When Asked for "10+2 / Intermediate / Higher Secondary Details":**
   * If there is a dropdown for qualification type: Select **"Diploma / 3-Year Polytechnic"**.
   * If there is no diploma option and 12th is marked with a mandatory asterisk (*): Select **"Others / Equivalent"** and type **"3-Year Diploma in Computer Science & Engineering"**.
   * Enter your Diploma Board (e.g., BTE / SBTE / State Board of Technical Education), Diploma roll number, aggregate marks, and passing year.
2. **When Asked for "B.Tech Semester Marks":**
   * For the Indian Army, Navy, and PSUs: You will be asked *"Are you a lateral entry student?"* Select **"YES"**.
   * The portal will automatically disable Semesters 1 & 2, or prompt you to enter marks starting directly from **Semester 3 to Semester 8**.
3. **Documents to Keep Ready for Upload:**
   * Class 10th Marksheet & Passing Certificate (Proof of Date of Birth).
   * 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma Consolidated Marksheet & Diploma Certificate.
   * B.Tech Semester Marksheets (Sem 3 to Sem 6/8) & Provisional Degree.
   * AICTE Approval Letter of your engineering college (standard document downloadable from your college website proving your Lateral Entry batch is AICTE approved).
