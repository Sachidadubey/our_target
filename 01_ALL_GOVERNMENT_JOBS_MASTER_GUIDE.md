## 1. Indian Army — Technical Graduate Course (TGC-145)

* **Organization & Post:** Indian Army, Directorate General of Recruiting | **Post:** Lieutenant (Permanent Commission in the Indian Army, Technical Arms/Signals).
* **Job Profile & Benefits:**
  * Commissioned as a Group 'A' Gazetted Military Officer. Work on battlefield military communications, tactical cyber operations, secure software systems, and electronic warfare.
  * **Benefits:** Free furnished military government accommodation/bungalow, dedicated orderly, full military hospital healthcare (ECHS) for self & dependents, subsidized CSD Canteen facilities, 60 days annual leave + 20 days casual leave, LTC free travel, free military ration, mess membership, lifelong military pension/NPS benefits.
* **Exact Eligibility:**
  * **Degree & Branch:** 4-year B.E. / B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering / Computer Technology / Information Technology. Final-year students (graduating 2026) and freshers are fully eligible.
  * **Age Limit:** 20 to 27 years as of 01 July 2027 (Born between 02 July 2000 and 01 July 2007).
  * **Marital Status:** Unmarried Male candidates.
  * **Experience Required:** 0 Years (Freshers eligible).
* **Salary Structure:**
  * **Pay Level:** Level 10 (Defence Pay Matrix: ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500).
  * **Basic Pay:** ₹56,100/month.
  * **Military Service Pay (MSP):** ₹15,500/month.
  * **Dearness Allowance (DA):** 50% = ₹35,800/month.
  * **Gross Monthly Salary:** **~₹1,15,000 – ₹1,25,000/month** + Field/High Altitude Allowances (₹10,500 – ₹25,000 extra when posted in operational areas).
  * **In-Hand Starting Salary:** ~₹98,000 – ₹1,05,000/month.
* **Amount to Pay for Apply:** **₹0 (Completely Free)**. No application fee. AC 3-Tier train fare refunded for attending first SSB interview.
* **Selection Process & Stages:**
  * **Stage 1 (Shortlisting):** Based on cumulative engineering marks up to 6th semester.
  * **Stage 2 (5-Day SSB Interview):**
    * *Day 1:* Screening Test — Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR) test + Picture Perception and Description Test (PPDT).
    * *Day 2:* Psychology Test — Thematic Apperception Test (TAT), Word Association Test (WAT), Situation Reaction Test (SRT), Self Description (SD).
    * *Day 3 & 4:* Group Testing Officer (GTO) Tasks — Group Discussion, Group Planning Exercise (GPE), Progressive Group Task (PGT), Half Group Task (HGT), Individual Obstacles, Command Task, Snake Race, Lecturette.
    * *Day 5:* Conference & Final Recommendation.
  * **Stage 3 (Special Medical Board):** Conducted at Military Hospitals.
* **Total Exam Marks & Exam Pattern:** No written examination. SSB Interview carries 900 marks (Screening, Psychology, GTO, Personal Interview).
* **Complete Syllabus:**
  * *OIR Test:* Verbal Reasoning (Coding, Analogies, Blood Relations, Number Series) and Non-Verbal Reasoning (Cube rotation, Pattern completion, Figure matrix).
  * *Lecturette Topics:* Geopolitics, AI in Defence, Cybersecurity, India's Semiconductor Mission, Space Tech.
* **Previous Cutoffs:**
  * Shortlisting cutoff for Computer Science: **74% to 78% cumulative aggregate marks** in B.Tech semesters.
* **Deadlines & Schedule:**
  * Application Opens: **30 September 2026**
  * Application Closes: **29 October 2026 (03:00 PM)**
  * SSB Interviews: **January – March 2027**
  * Course Commences: **July 2027 at Indian Military Academy (IMA), Dehradun**
* **Direct Official Links:**
  * **Apply Online:** [https://joinindianarmy.nic.in/login.htm](https://joinindianarmy.nic.in/login.htm)
  * **Official Portal:** [https://joinindianarmy.nic.in](https://joinindianarmy.nic.in)
* **Exact Documents Required:**
  * Class 10th Certificate & Marksheet (DOB proof).
  * Class 12th Certificate & Marksheet.
  * B.Tech Semester Marksheets (Sem 1 to 6/8 merged PDF < 1 MB).
  * CGPA-to-Percentage conversion certificate issued by University.
  * Scanned photograph (white background, 20–50 KB, JPEG).
  * Scanned signature (10–20 KB, JPEG).

  * **CBT-2 (120 Marks, 90 Minutes):**
    * General Awareness: 50 Questions (50 Marks)
    * Mathematics: 35 Questions (35 Marks)
    * General Intelligence & Reasoning: 35 Questions (35 Marks)
    * Negative Marking: 1/3rd mark deducted per wrong answer.
* **Complete Syllabus:**
  * *Mathematics:* Number System, Decimals, Fractions, LCM/HCF, Ratio and Proportion, Percentages, Mensuration, Time and Work, Time and Distance, Simple and Compound Interest, Profit and Loss, Elementary Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Elementary Statistics.
  * *Reasoning:* Analogies, Completion of Number and Alphabetical Series, Coding and Decoding, Mathematical Operations, Similarities and Differences, Relationships, Analytical Reasoning, Syllogism, Jumbling, Venn Diagrams, Puzzle, Data Sufficiency, Statement-Conclusion, Decision Making.
  * *General Awareness:* Current Events of National and International Importance, Games and Sports, Art and Culture of India, Indian Literature, Monuments and Places of India, General Science and Life Science (up to 10th CBSE), History of India and Freedom Struggle, Physical, Social and Economic Geography of India and World, Indian Polity and Governance- constitution and political system, General Scientific and Technological Developments including Space and Nuclear Program of India, Environmental issues, Basics of Computers and Computer Applications.
* **Previous Cutoffs (Normalized CBT-2 Scores out of 100):**
  * Station Master (Level 6): **UR: 72.45 – 76.12 | EWS: 62.80 – 66.40 | OBC: 68.20 – 72.10 | SC: 58.30 | ST: 54.10**
  * Goods Train Manager (Level 5): **UR: 74.20 – 78.50 | EWS: 65.10 – 69.20 | OBC: 70.40 – 73.80**
* **Deadlines & Schedule:**
  * Application Opens: **08 October 2026**
