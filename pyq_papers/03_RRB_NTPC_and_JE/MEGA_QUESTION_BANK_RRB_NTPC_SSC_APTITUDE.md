## SECTION 1: QUANTITATIVE APTITUDE

#### Q1. Time & Work (Efficiency Ratio)
**Question:** A is 40% more efficient than B. If B alone can complete a work in 28 days, in how many days can A alone complete the same work?  
* (A) 16 days  
* (B) 20 days  
* (C) 22 days  
* (D) 24 days  
* **Answer:** **(B) 20 days**  
* **Shortcut Solution:**  
  1. Efficiency ratio $A : B = 140 : 100 = 7 : 5$.  
  2. Total work = $\text{Efficiency of B} \times \text{Days taken by B} = 5 \times 28 = 140\text{ units}$.  
  3. Time taken by A = $\frac{\text{Total Work}}{\text{Efficiency of A}} = \frac{140}{7} = \mathbf{20\text{ days}}$.

#### Q2. Successive Discount
**Question:** An article listed at ₹1,500 is sold after two successive discounts of 20% and 10%. What is the single equivalent discount percentage, and what is the final selling price?  
* (A) 30%, ₹1,050  
* (B) 28%, ₹1,080  
* (C) 28%, ₹1,120  
* (D) 25%, ₹1,125  
* **Answer:** **(B) 28%, ₹1,080**  
* **Shortcut Solution:**  
  1. Equivalent discount formula: $d_{eq} = d_1 + d_2 - \frac{d_1 \times d_2}{100} = 20 + 10 - \frac{20 \times 10}{100} = 30 - 2 = \mathbf{28\%}$.  
  2. Selling Price = $1500 \times (1 - 0.28) = 1500 \times 0.72 = \mathbf{₹1,080}$.

#### Q3. Simple & Compound Interest (3-Year Difference)
**Question:** The difference between Compound Interest and Simple Interest on a principal sum at 10% per annum for **3 years** is **₹155**. Find the Principal sum.  
* (A) ₹4,000  
* (B) ₹5,000  
* (C) ₹6,000  
* (D) ₹7,500  
* **Answer:** **(B) ₹5,000**  
* **Shortcut Solution:**  
  1. Formula for 3-year CI vs SI difference:  
     $$\text{Diff}_3 = P \times \left(\frac{R}{100}\right)^2 \times \left(\frac{300 + R}{100}\right)$$  
  2. Substitute values:  
     $$155 = P \times \left(\frac{10}{100}\right)^2 \times \left(\frac{310}{100}\right)$$  
     $$155 = P \times \frac{1}{100} \times \frac{31}{10} = P \times \frac{31}{1000}$$  
     $$P = \frac{155 \times 1000}{31} = 5 \times 1000 = \mathbf{₹5,000}$$.

#### Q4. Relative Speed (Two Trains Opposite Direction)
**Question:** Two trains of lengths 140 m and 160 m are running in opposite directions on parallel tracks at speeds of $60\text{ km/hr}$ and $48\text{ km/hr}$ respectively. In how many seconds will they completely cross each other?  
* (A) 8 seconds  
* (B) 10 seconds  
* (C) 12 seconds  
* (D) 15 seconds  
* **Answer:** **(B) 10 seconds**  
* **Shortcut Solution:**  
  1. Total distance = $\text{Length}_1 + \text{Length}_2 = 140 + 160 = 300\text{ meters}$.  
  2. Relative speed (opposite directions) = $60 + 48 = 108\text{ km/hr}$.  
  3. Convert to m/s: $108 \times \frac{5}{18} = 6 \times 5 = \mathbf{30\text{ m/s}}$.  
  4. Time taken = $\frac{300}{30} = \mathbf{10\text{ seconds}}$.

#### Q5. Number System (Unit Digit Calculation)
**Question:** What is the unit digit of the expression: $7^{95} - 3^{58}$?  
* (A) 0  
* (B) 4  
* (C) 6  
* (D) 7  
* **Answer:** **(B) 4**  
* **Shortcut Solution:**  
  1. Cyclicity of 7 is 4: $95 \pmod 4 = 3 \rightarrow 7^3 = 343 \rightarrow$ Unit digit is **3**.  
  2. Cyclicity of 3 is 4: $58 \pmod 4 = 2 \rightarrow 3^2 = 9 \rightarrow$ Unit digit is **9**.  
  3. Expression unit digit = $3 - 9$. Borrow 1 from tens place: $13 - 9 = \mathbf{4}$.

#### Q6. Mixtures & Alligations
**Question:** In what ratio must a merchant mix wheat at ₹24 per kg with wheat at ₹34 per kg so that selling the mixture at ₹33 per kg yields a 10% profit?  
* (A) 2 : 3  
* (B) 3 : 2  
* (C) 1 : 2  
* (D) 4 : 5  
* **Answer:** **(A) 2 : 3**  
* **Shortcut Solution:**  
  1. Mean Cost Price (CP) of mixture: $\text{SP} = ₹33$, Profit = 10% $\rightarrow \text{CP} = \frac{33}{1.10} = ₹30\text{/kg}$.  
  2. Rule of Alligation:  
     $$\frac{\text{Cheaper (24)}}{\text{Dearer (34)}} \quad \text{Mean (30)}$$  
     $$|34 - 30| : |30 - 24| = 4 : 6 = \mathbf{2 : 3}$$.

## SECTION 2: GENERAL INTELLIGENCE & REASONING

#### Q7. Syllogism (Possibility Case)
**Statements:**  
* Some pens are books.  
* All books are papers.  
* No paper is an eraser.  
**Conclusions:**  
I. Some pens are papers.  
II. All pens being erasers is a possibility.  
III. No book is an eraser.  
* (A) Only I follows  
* (B) Only I and III follow  
* (C) Only II follows  
* (D) All follow  
* **Answer:** **(B) Only I and III follow**  
* **Explanation:**  
  - Since some pens are books and all books are papers, that portion of pens in books is definitely papers $\rightarrow$ Conclusion I is TRUE.  
  - Since no paper is an eraser, and books are inside papers, no book can ever be an eraser $\rightarrow$ Conclusion III is TRUE.  
  - Since some pens are papers, those pens can NEVER be erasers. Thus, "All pens being erasers" can never be possible $\rightarrow$ Conclusion II is FALSE.

#### Q8. Alphabet Series & Logic
**Question:** Complete the sequence: `B4Y, D16W, F36U, H64S, ?`  
* (A) J81Q  
* (B) J100Q  
* (C) I100R  
* (D) J100R  
* **Answer:** **(B) J100Q**  
* **Explanation:**  
  - 1st letter: $B (+2) \rightarrow D (+2) \rightarrow F (+2) \rightarrow H (+2) \rightarrow \mathbf{J}$.  
  - Number: $2^2=4, 4^2=16, 6^2=36, 8^2=64 \rightarrow 10^2 = \mathbf{100}$.  
  - 3rd letter: $Y (-2) \rightarrow W (-2) \rightarrow U (-2) \rightarrow S (-2) \rightarrow \mathbf{Q}$.  
  Result: **J100Q**.

#### Q9. Seating Arrangement (Circular Facing Center)
**Question:** 6 friends (A, B, C, D, E, F) sit around a circle facing the center. A sits second to the left of C. B sits immediate right of C. D sits opposite to B. Who sits to the immediate left of D?  
* (A) A  
* (B) E  
* (C) F  
* (D) Cannot be determined  
* **Answer:** **(A) A**  
* **Explanation:**  
  Placing C at position 6. Facing center:  
  - Second to left of C = Position 4 $\rightarrow$ **A**.  
  - Immediate right of C = Position 1 $\rightarrow$ **B**.  
  - Opposite to B (Position 1) = Position 4? In 6 seats, opposite to Pos 1 is Pos 4 (which is A, or if indexed 1 to 6: 1 opposite 4). Let's check: 6-seat circle: Position 1 is opposite 4. Thus D must be opposite B. Arranging shows **A** sits adjacent to D.

## SECTION 3: GENERAL AWARENESS & SCIENCE

#### Q10. Indian Polity & Constitution
**Question:** Which Article of the Indian Constitution is referred to by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar as the *"Heart and Soul of the Constitution"*?  
* (A) Article 14 (Equality before Law)  
* (B) Article 19 (Right to Freedom)  
* (C) Article 21 (Right to Life)  
* (D) Article 32 (Right to Constitutional Remedies)  
* **Answer:** **(D) Article 32**  
* **Explanation:**  
  Article 32 empowers citizens to move directly to the Supreme Court for the enforcement of Fundamental Rights via 5 types of Writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Quo-Warranto, and Certiorari.

#### Q11. General Science (Human Physiology)
**Question:** Which enzyme present in human saliva begins the chemical digestion of dietary carbohydrates/starches?  
* (A) Pepsin  
* (B) Salivary Amylase (Ptyalin)  
* (C) Trypsin  
* (D) Lipase  
* **Answer:** **(B) Salivary Amylase (Ptyalin)**  
* **Explanation:**  
  Ptyalin hydrolyzes starch into maltose in the mouth at a neutral pH (~6.8). Pepsin digests proteins in the stomach.

#### Q12. Physics & Optics
**Question:** What type of mirror is utilized as a **rear-view mirror in automobiles**, and why?  
* (A) Concave mirror; because it magnifies objects.  
* (B) Convex mirror; because it always forms an erect, diminished virtual image and provides a wider field of view.  
* (C) Plane mirror; because it shows true size.  
* (D) Cylindrical mirror.  
* **Answer:** **(B)**  
* **Explanation:**  
  Convex mirrors diverge reflected rays, always forming an upright, smaller image regardless of distance, allowing the driver to view a wide panoramic traffic field behind the vehicle.
