### Set A: Simplification & Approximation (Solve in under 30 seconds each)

**Q1. Find the value of $x$:**
$$\sqrt{784} \div 4 + (18 \times 12) - \sqrt{x} = 215$$
- **Option 1:** 64
- **Option 2:** 49
- **Option 3:** 81
- **Option 4:** 100
- **Correct Answer:** Option 1 (64)
- **Shortcut Solution:**
  1. $\sqrt{784} = 28$. Then $28 \div 4 = 7$.
  2. $18 \times 12 = 18 \times (10 + 2) = 180 + 36 = 216$.
  3. $7 + 216 - \sqrt{x} = 215 \implies 223 - \sqrt{x} = 215 \implies \sqrt{x} = 223 - 215 = 8$.
  4. $x = 8^2 = 64$. (Time taken: 18 seconds).

**Q2. Find the value of $x$:**
$$(25\% \text{ of } 720) + (15\% \text{ of } 480) - (12 \times 8) = x$$
- **Option 1:** 156
- **Option 2:** 162
- **Option 3:** 148
- **Option 4:** 150
- **Correct Answer:** Option 1 (156)
- **Shortcut Solution:**
  1. $25\% \text{ of } 720 = \frac{1}{4} \times 720 = 180$.
  2. $15\% \text{ of } 480 = 10\% (48) + 5\% (24) = 72$.
  3. $12 \times 8 = 96$.
  4. $x = 180 + 72 - 96 = 252 - 96 = 156$. (Time taken: 20 seconds).

**Q3. Quadratic Equation (Sign Method):**
- Equation I: $x^2 - 11x + 30 = 0$
- Equation II: $y^2 - 17y + 72 = 0$
- **Comparison options:** (a) $x > y$, (b) $x < y$, (c) $x \ge y$, (d) $x \le y$, (e) $x = y$ or relationship cannot be established.
- **Correct Answer:** (b) $x < y$
- **Sign Method Solution:**
  1. Equation I: Signs of $(b, c) = (-, +) \implies$ Roots are $(+, +)$.
     Factors of 30 that add to 11 are $6$ and $5$. Roots $x = +6, +5$.
  2. Equation II: Signs of $(b, c) = (-, +) \implies$ Roots are $(+, +)$.
     Factors of 72 that add to 17 are $9$ and $8$. Roots $y = +9, +8$.
  3. Compare: $6 < 9, 6 < 8$, and $5 < 9, 5 < 8$.
  4. Therefore, $x < y$. (Time taken: 15 seconds).

## Part 2: High-Yield Reasoning (Actual Exam Sets)

### Syllogism (Modern "Only a few" pattern)

**Statements:**
- Only a few Pens are Pencils. (Means: Some Pens are Pencils AND Some Pens are NOT Pencils).
- All Pencils are Erasers.
- No Eraser is a Scale.

**Conclusions:**
- I. All Pens can never be Scales.
- II. Some Pens are Erasers.
- III. All Pencils being Scale is a possibility.

**Analysis & Solution:**
1. Check Conclusion I: "All Pens can never be Scales."
   - The common portion between Pens and Pencils is inside Erasers.
   - Since No Eraser is Scale, that common portion of Pens can never be Scale! Hence, True.
2. Check Conclusion II: "Some Pens are Erasers."
   - Since Some Pens are Pencils, and All Pencils are Erasers, those Pens must be Erasers. Hence, True.
3. Check Conclusion III: "All Pencils being Scale is a possibility."
   - Since All Pencils are inside Erasers, and No Eraser can be Scale, No Pencil can ever be Scale. Hence, False.
- **Answer:** Only Conclusions I and II follow. (Time taken: 40 seconds).

### Inequality (Open Gate Trick)

**Statement:**
$$P \ge Q > R = S \le T < U$$

**Conclusions:**
- I. $P > S$
- II. $T \ge R$

**Solution:**
1. Check I ($P > S$): Can we travel from $P$ to $S$?
   - $P \ge Q$ (gate open)
   - $Q > R$ (gate open, strict inequality $>$ encountered!)
   - $R = S$ (gate open)
   - We reached $S$ and crossed at least one strict sign ($>$). $\implies$ **Conclusion I is TRUE.**
2. Check II ($T \ge R$): Can we travel from $T$ to $R$?
   - $T \ge S$ (from $S \le T$, gate open)
   - $S = R$ (gate open)
   - Both signs are $\ge$ and $=$. $\implies$ **Conclusion II is TRUE.**
- **Answer:** Both Conclusions I and II are True. (Time taken: 20 seconds).

## Part 3: RRB JE CBT-1 General Science (Top Repeated Questions)

**Q1. An electric bulb is rated 220 V and 100 W. When it is operated on 110 V, the power consumed will be:**
- **Option 1:** 100 W
- **Option 2:** 75 W
- **Option 3:** 50 W
- **Option 4:** 25 W
- **Correct Answer:** Option 4 (25 W)
- **Explanation:**
  - Resistance of bulb: $R = \frac{V^2}{P} = \frac{220^2}{100} = \frac{48400}{100} = 484\ \Omega$.
  - Power consumed at $110\text{ V}$: $P' = \frac{V'^2}{R} = \frac{110^2}{484} = \frac{12100}{484} = 25\text{ W}$.
